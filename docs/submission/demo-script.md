# Demo script — Tranche (1:41)

Recorded at 1920×1080 with a synthetic voiceover and burned-in subtitles. The
recording is generated by `open-video-demo` from the scene script in
`script/story.json`; the narration below is the exact voiceover.

**Video:** `docs/submission/tranche-demo.mp4`
**Subtitles:** `docs/submission/tranche-demo.srt`

## 0:00 — Title

**On screen:** Tranche title card, green rail, the product name, and the line
“Pay when it's proven.”

**Narration:** An agent can move money in seconds. The harder question is whether
the work was actually done. Tranche turns a milestone into a test the agent has
to pass before it gets paid.

## 0:11 — The refusal

**On screen:** The landing page’s “The payment that doesn’t happen” section. The
padded claim sits next to the proven work sample.

**Narration:** The useful part of a payment demo is the refusal. A claim can look
finished while the demo link is dead or the numbers are made up. Someone has to
check before the money leaves.

## 0:22 — The release room

**On screen:** The console, scrolled to the milestone list. Two blocked bounties
and two released builds, including the 0.10 USDC Kite settlement.

**Narration:** The release room lists every milestone and every decision. Two
bounties were blocked. Two releases settled. The small one settled through Kite
Passport, on Base.

## 0:33 — A milestone that passed

**On screen:** The Kite Passport milestone page. The acceptance graph passes; the
verdict panel shows SERV and Reppo both accepting.

**Narration:** Here is a milestone that passed. The repository is public, the demo
responds, and the claim matches the links. SERV reads the claim. Reppo evaluates
it independently. Both accept, and the policy releases the tranche.

## 0:48 — The live settlement

**On screen:** The public receipt. The settlement section shows
`kite_passport · settled` and the Base transaction hash.

**Narration:** This is the settlement. Ten cents, real money, on Base. The receipt
shows the rail, the amount, and the transaction hash. The payout was created by
the app, approved with a passkey, and recorded only after the signed send came
back.

## 1:04 — The refusal, with proof

**On screen:** The blocked receipt. Both verdicts are reject; the settlement
section shows `ledger · blocked`, reference not issued, and 0.00 USDC moved.

**Narration:** Now the refusal. This claim says the demo works and the numbers are
real. The link returns a 404. The numbers are not in the README. SERV rejects it.
Reppo rejects it too. Zero dollars moved, and the receipt says why.

## 1:20 — The ceiling

**On screen:** The over-ceiling milestone. The payment is held at 500 USDC against
a 100 USDC auto-release ceiling, with the operator approval control visible.

**Narration:** The policy also has a ceiling. This tranche is five hundred dollars,
above the limit. Even with a passing verdict, the payment waits for a human. The
model never gets to move money on its own.

## 1:32 — Close

**On screen:** Tranche close card with the live demo, repository, and the live
receipt reference.

**Narration:** Tranche is the acceptance layer for agent payments. The agent can
pay. The proof decides whether it should. Pay when it's proven.
